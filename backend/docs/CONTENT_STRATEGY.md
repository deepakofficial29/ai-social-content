# Content Strategy

`build_generation_plan` combines:

- Platform and custom platform description.
- Content objective.
- Target audience.
- Tone and language.
- Media type and evidence package.
- Direct observations and interpretations.
- Contradictions and uncertainty.

The plan contains the main angle, key message, audience relevance, structure, tone instructions, platform considerations, supported facts, evidence IDs, and prohibited claims.

Interpretations and unresolved conflicts are not promoted to direct facts.
